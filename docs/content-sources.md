## Editorial decisions

- The official site's hours conflict across pages (Monday–Friday versus Monday–Saturday), so no hours are displayed.
- No numerical review rating, testimonial, customer logo, guarantee, emergency dispatch promise, project count or tenure claim is used.
- No claim about current licensing or insurance status is made. These would require current authoritative verification before public launch.
- Services and geographic coverage reflect published company information. Each inquiry asks the team to confirm scope and availability.
- Generated photos are explicitly illustrative, not staff portraits or portfolio evidence.
- Copy is original, written for this proposed site. No reviews are reproduced.
